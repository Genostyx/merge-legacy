# Changelog

Player-facing release notes, in the form the App Store and Play Console want:
short, plain, about what changed for the person playing. Paste the section for
a version straight into the store listing's "What's New" field.

Keep entries under about 500 characters - Play Console's limit - and leave the
engineering detail in the commit messages, where it belongs.

Newest first.

## Unreleased

- Crates are bigger, sit properly in their square, and bronze looks like metal
  instead of a cardboard box.
- Energy bolts are sized to match the coins and gems beside them, and board
  items now catch the light the same way the ones in the top bar do.
- The water source's parts are redrawn: a well ring, a support frame, a roof
  and a winch, instead of lengths of pipe.
- The player profile is tidier - no title bar eating the top, a bigger level
  display, and daily rewards you can actually read.
- The shop no longer closes when you tap a blank part of it.

## v0.2.0-beta

- Board pieces are noticeably bigger and fill their squares, so items are
  easier to tell apart at a glance.
- Tapping an item no longer picks it up by accident - a piece only moves once
  you actually drag it.
- The top bar lines up: level badge, currency chips, settings and shop all sit
  on one line.
- Living room stages open with a clearer list of what each one needs.

## v0.1.0-beta

First test build.

- Merge board with wood, stone, glass and water chains, dispensers, orders,
  energy and crates.
- The living room project, furnished one piece at a time in real 3D, with a
  camera you can move.
- Collection, daily rewards, shop and supply crates.
- Fullscreen from the settings gear, and the game remembers your progress
  between visits.
